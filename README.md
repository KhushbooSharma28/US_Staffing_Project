# US_Staffing_Project
This project analyzes the performance of a US IT staffing company that works exclusively with prime vendors. The analysis covers the period from January to June and focuses on measuring recruiter and Business Development Manager (BDM) performance, hiring trends, revenue, and gross margin. The problem here was Delivery head unable to track the performance of Recruiters and BDMs manually, revenue of half of the year, gross magin. 

The project demonstrates how Python, SQL, and Power BI can be used together to clean, analyze, and visualize staffing data for business decision-making.
